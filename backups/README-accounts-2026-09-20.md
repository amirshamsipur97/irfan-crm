# Accounts standardisation, 2026-09-20

Backup of every row touched when the agent-made Accounts rows were merged into
the register standard (developer companies = Accounts, projects = Developments).

`crm-accounts-standardisation-2026-09-20.json` holds, as they were BEFORE the
merge: the 14 non-developer accounts, the 35 contacts and 5 deals pointing at
them, and the contacts board's custom columns.

To undo a row, write its `account_id` / `account_name` / `custom` back.
